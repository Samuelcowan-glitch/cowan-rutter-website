Description

A bright and characterful self-contained office arranged across the first and second floors of 416 King’s Road, in the heart of Chelsea. The accommodation provides flexible workspace across two well-proportioned floors, allowing an incoming occupier to create a combination of open-plan working areas, private offices and meeting space. Large windows provide an abundance of natural light, creating a bright and comfortable working environment throughout. The premises benefit from their own pantry, WC and shower facilities, making the property particularly well suited to businesses looking for practical, self-contained accommodation.

Location

The property is prominently positioned on the southern side of King’s Road, close to the junctions with Beaufort Street and Lamont Road, in the heart of Chelsea’s well-established design and commercial district. King’s Road is internationally recognised for its distinctive mix of independent boutiques, interior-design showrooms, cafés, restaurants and established businesses. The surrounding area is home to a strong community of creative, professional and design-led occupiers.
