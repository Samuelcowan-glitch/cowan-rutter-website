Description

A prominent double-fronted commercial unit available to let at 96–98 Waterford Road, Fulham, London SW6. The property provides approximately 1,933 sq ft of accommodation arranged across the ground and lower-ground floors. The ground floor offers approximately 948 sq ft of bright, spacious retail or showroom space, benefitting from an impressive glazed frontage and large display windows visible from King’s Road. The substantial lower-ground floor extends to approximately 916 sq ft and provides flexible additional showroom, office or storage accommodation, together with approximately 68 sq ft of vault space. The premises would suit a range of retail, showroom, design-led and professional occupiers seeking a prominent commercial property close to King’s Road and Chelsea’s established design district.

Location

The property is situated on the western side of Waterford Road, close to its junction with King’s Road and within the Chelsea Design Quarter. The surrounding area is well established as a destination for furniture, interiors, lighting and design businesses, with Chelsea Harbour and the Design Centre also nearby. Fulham Broadway Underground station is within easy walking distance, providing District line connections into central London. Numerous bus routes operate along King’s Road, serving Chelsea, Fulham and the wider southwest London area.
