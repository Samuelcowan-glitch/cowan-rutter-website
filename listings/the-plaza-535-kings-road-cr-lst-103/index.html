Location

The property is prominently situated at The Plaza, 535 King’s Road, within the established Chelsea Design Quarter. The area is renowned for its concentration of luxury furniture, interior design and specialist showroom businesses, making it an excellent location for occupiers seeking a strong presence in Chelsea.
