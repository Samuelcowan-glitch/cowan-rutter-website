Description

A rarely available, self-contained commercial unit extending to approximately 1,590 sq ft and arranged entirely over the ground floor. Formed from two combined units, the premises provide predominantly open-plan accommodation with a separate area to the rear incorporating WC facilities. The flexible layout offers scope for adaptation to suit a variety of educational, community, office, studio, medical or other commercial uses, subject to the property’s existing lawful use and any necessary planning consent. The property benefits from prominent street frontage on Dawes Road and is well positioned to serve the densely populated residential areas of Fulham.

Location

The property is situated on the southern side of Dawes Road, close to its junction with Varna Road and approximately 10 minutes’ walk from Fulham Broadway. The surrounding area comprises a busy mix of residential accommodation, independent retailers, cafés, professional services and community facilities. Fulham Broadway provides a wider range of shopping, restaurant and leisure amenities, together with District line services connecting the property to Chelsea, Earl’s Court and Central London.
