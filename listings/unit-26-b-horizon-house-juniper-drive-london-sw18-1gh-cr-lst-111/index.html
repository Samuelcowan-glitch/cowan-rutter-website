Description

A well-presented furnished office to let in Horizon House, Battersea Reach, providing approximately 1,085 sq ft (101 sq m) of modern ground-floor commercial accommodation. The office is arranged to provide a practical and professional working environment and benefits from a private meeting room, air conditioning and two WCs. The premises are offered furnished, allowing an incoming occupier to establish themselves with minimal fit-out requirements. The development provides 24-hour concierge and a range of convenient on-site amenities, including coffee shops, a bar and restaurant and Tesco Express.
