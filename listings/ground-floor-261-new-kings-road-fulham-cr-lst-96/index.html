Description

A well-positioned commercial unit extending to approximately 914 sq ft, arranged over the ground and lower-ground floors. The ground floor provides prominent customer-facing accommodation with a glazed shopfront onto New King’s Road, while the lower-ground floor offers useful ancillary, storage or workspace accommodation. The premises would suit a variety of retail, beauty, professional-service, office or studio occupiers within Class E, subject to the property’s existing lawful use and any necessary consents. There may also be longer-term potential for residential conversion, subject to planning permission.

Location

The property occupies a prominent position on the southern side of New King’s Road in Fulham, close to Fulham High Street and Putney Bridge. This established commercial stretch serves an affluent and densely populated residential catchment and is home to a varied mix of independent retailers, cafés, restaurants, salons and professional businesses. Putney Bridge Underground station is within easy walking distance, providing District line services into Central London. Parsons Green station is also accessible, while numerous bus routes operate along New King’s Road and provide connections throughout Fulham, Chelsea and southwest London.
