Description

A modern and well-presented ground-floor studio apartment extending to approximately 390 sq ft within the well-maintained Regency House. The apartment has been finished to a contemporary standard and offers a bright, thoughtfully arranged living and sleeping area, a modern kitchen and bathroom, and the added benefit of a separate storage space. The property is offered fully furnished and is ready for immediate occupation. Location Regency House is conveniently located close to Fulham Broadway, providing easy access to Fulham Broadway Underground Station and numerous bus routes. The property is within walking distance of the excellent shops, cafés, restaurants and leisure facilities along Fulham Road and within Fulham Broadway. The open spaces of Eel Brook Common and Parsons Green are also nearby.
