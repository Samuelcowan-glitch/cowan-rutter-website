Location

The property is prominently situated on New Kings Road in Parsons Green, one of Fulham’s most established and sought-after commercial and residential areas. The surrounding area is home to a strong mix of independent retailers, cafés, restaurants and professional businesses, supported by an affluent local population. Parsons Green Underground Station provides District line services and is within a short walk, while several bus routes operate along New Kings Road, providing convenient connections across Fulham and into Central London.
