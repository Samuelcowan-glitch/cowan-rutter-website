Location

Plato Place occupies an excellent position in the heart of Fulham, one of South West London's most sought-after business and residential districts. Situated just off Fulham Road on St. Dionis Road, the property is within a short walk of Parsons Green Underground Station (District Line), providing direct access to Central London, Victoria and the City. Fulham Broadway is also nearby, offering further transport links and a wide range of amenities. The surrounding area is home to an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants, independent retailers and fitness facilities, together with Chelsea & Westminster Hospital and a variety of established professional businesses. New King's Road, Fulham Road and King's Road are all within easy reach, making the location highly attractive for occupiers seeking a well-connected office in an established West London business environment.
