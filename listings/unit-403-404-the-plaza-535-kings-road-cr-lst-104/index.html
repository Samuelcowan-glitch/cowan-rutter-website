Description

Units 4.03–4.04 provide approximately 1,130 sq ft of combined office accommodation within the established Plaza 535 commercial development. The premises offer flexible workspace suitable for open-plan desks, private offices and meeting areas. The offices form part of a purpose-built courtyard development and benefit from excellent natural light, passenger-lift access, 24-hour access and security, and on-site parking. The attractive landscaped courtyard provides an excellent communal area for informal meetings and breaks.

Location

The Plaza occupies a prominent position at the junction of King’s Road and Lots Road in Chelsea, close to the specialist showrooms and creative businesses of the Chelsea Design Quarter. Imperial Wharf station is within approximately five minutes’ walk, providing London Overground connections to Clapham Junction and Shepherd’s Bush. Fulham Broadway Underground station is also readily accessible, while regular bus services along King’s Road provide convenient connections to Sloane Square and central London. The surrounding area offers an excellent selection of cafés, restaurants, shops and leisure facilities.
