Description

The accommodation provides bright, well-presented workspace with an excellent balance of open-plan offices, private meeting rooms and individual offices, making it ideal for professional practices, creative businesses, consultancies and a wide variety of Class E occupiers. Benefitting from excellent natural light throughout, the floor offers flexible accommodation that can be adapted to suit a range of business requirements, whilst retaining the privacy and functionality of separate working areas. Kitchen and WC facilities are located on the floor, allowing an occupier to enjoy fully self-contained office accommodation. All Utilities, Building insurance, communal area cleaning and other sundries will be included in the additional 6 monthly service charge. Business rates are payable by the tenant.

Location

Britannia House occupies a prominent position on Britannia Way, just off King's Road in the heart of Chelsea. The surrounding area offers an excellent mix of independent cafés, restaurants, gyms, retailers and everyday amenities, creating an attractive working environment for both staff and clients. The property is within easy walking distance of Fulham Broadway Underground Station (District Line) and Imperial Wharf Station (London Overground), providing excellent connectivity across London. King's Road, Chelsea Harbour and the River Thames are all close by, while the nearby A4 offers convenient access to Central London, Heathrow Airport and the wider motorway network.
