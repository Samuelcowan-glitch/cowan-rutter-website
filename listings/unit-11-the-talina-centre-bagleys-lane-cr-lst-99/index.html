Description

The Talina Centre is one of the few Industrial / Warehousing developments within the London Borough of Hammersmith & Fulham where units are used for Warehousing & Light Industrial purpose. The premises are on 2 floors and extend to approximately 1,765 sq ft Gross Internal with a useable net area of approx. 1,570 Sq Ft once the stairs and WC’s have been excluded. The units were originally built as full height industrial units with a small first floor office and a roller shutter to the ground floor. The first floor has been extended over the entire area to create the 2 storey unit. There have been substantial upgrades to the unit as per below:- Installation of a cross steel to fully open up the ground floor Conversion of a WC to a kitchen Provision of a new boiler Decorations and floor coverings throughout Installation of a sophisticated security system Newly installed roller shutter door The unit has private on site parking facilities for cars and bicycles.

Location

The Talina Centre is situated on the east side of Bagleys Lane close to the junction with Townmead Road. Imperial Wharf Station is close by providing a rail link from Clapham Junction in the south and West Brompton (District Line), Shepherds Bush (Central Line) and Willesden Junction. Fulham Broadway LRT is also within walking distance.
