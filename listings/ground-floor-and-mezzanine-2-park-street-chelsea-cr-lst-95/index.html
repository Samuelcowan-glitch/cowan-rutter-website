Description

A prominent and versatile commercial unit extending to approximately 1,564 sq ft, arranged over the ground floor and mezzanine within the prestigious Chelsea Creek development. The premises benefit from an impressive fully glazed frontage, excellent ceiling height and flexible shell-and-core accommodation with capped services, providing an occupier with the opportunity to create a bespoke fit-out. The unit would suit a range of retail, showroom, café, restaurant, professional-service or other commercial occupiers, subject to the property’s existing lawful use and any necessary consents.

Location

The property is situated within Chelsea Creek, a prominent mixed-use waterside development positioned between Chelsea Harbour, Imperial Wharf and the River Thames. Chelsea Creek and the surrounding Imperial Wharf development contain a substantial and affluent residential population, together with offices, restaurants, cafés, hotels and leisure amenities. Nearby destinations include the Design Centre Chelsea Harbour, Chelsea Harbour Club and Chelsea Harbour Hotel. Imperial Wharf station is within easy walking distance and provides London Overground and Southern services to Clapham Junction, West Brompton, Shepherd’s Bush and Willesden Junction. Chelsea Harbour Pier also offers riverboat connections via Uber Boat by Thames Clippers.
