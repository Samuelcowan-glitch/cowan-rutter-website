Description

Harbour Yard offers a flexible selection of modern office spaces within the prestigious Chelsea Harbour development in London SW10. Available offices range from approximately 538 sq ft to 1,665 sq ft, providing suitable options for small, medium-sized and established businesses. The accommodation includes a variety of layouts, with both conventional and flexible office solutions available, including spacious duplex units. The offices provide bright and professional working environments, with selected units benefitting from air conditioning, fitted kitchens, meeting areas and views across Chelsea Harbour and the surrounding riverside area. Furnished and unfurnished options may be available, allowing incoming occupiers to select accommodation that suits their operational requirements.

Location

Harbour Yard is situated within Chelsea Harbour, a prestigious mixed-use riverside development close to King’s Road and the River Thames. The development is home to the internationally recognised Design Centre, Chelsea Harbour, alongside a range of cafés, restaurants, showrooms, offices and a luxury hotel. The location has become particularly popular with businesses operating in the design, interiors, architecture and creative sectors. Imperial Wharf station is moments from the property, providing London Overground and National Rail connections to Clapham Junction, West Brompton, Shepherd’s Bush and the wider London transport network. Numerous bus routes also provide convenient access to Chelsea, Fulham and central London.
