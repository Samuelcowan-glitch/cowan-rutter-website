Description

A substantial and versatile commercial unit extending to approximately 1,499 sq ft, arranged over the ground floor, mezzanine and lower-ground floor. The premises are currently configured to provide six treatment rooms, together with a kitchen and WC facilities, and offer excellent scope for reconfiguration to suit a variety of retail, showroom, studio, office or professional service occupiers, subject to the necessary consents. The ground floor benefits from prominent street frontage and a glazed shopfront, providing good visibility onto Wandsworth Bridge Road. The split-level arrangement offers flexible accommodation suitable for both customer-facing and ancillary uses.

Location

The property is prominently situated on the eastern side of Wandsworth Bridge Road, close to its junction with Ryecroft Street and a short distance south of New King’s Road. Wandsworth Bridge Road is a popular and established commercial destination serving the surrounding affluent residential areas of Fulham, Parsons Green and Sands End. The area is home to a diverse mix of independent retailers, cafés, restaurants, interior-design businesses and professional services, with nearby occupiers including Caffè Nero, Neptune, Hector Finch and Sainsbury’s Local. Fulham Broadway and Parsons Green Underground stations are both within convenient walking distance, providing District line services. Numerous local bus routes also offer connections throughout Fulham, Chelsea, Hammersmith and Central London.
