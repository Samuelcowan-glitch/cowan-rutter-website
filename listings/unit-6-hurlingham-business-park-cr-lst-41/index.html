Description

The property forms part of Hurlingham Business Park, a purpose-built commercial development comprising warehouse, studio and office units. The accommodation extends to approximately 2,867 sq ft (266.35 sq m) across the ground and first floors. The 1,247 sq ft ground floor provides a versatile open-plan warehouse or studio area with double-opening fold-back loading doors. The 1,620 sq ft first floor provides office accommodation with a modern fitted kitchen and WC facilities. The unit benefits from ample off-street parking, one designated parking space and a 24-hour manned security lodge. The premises are suitable for warehouse, light industrial, studio, showroom and office uses, subject to the necessary consents.

Key Terms

  • Investment opportunity in the heart of Fulham, Strong reversionary potential, Ample parking

Location

Hurlingham Business Park is situated on Sulivan Road which runs between Peterborough Road and Broomhouse Lane. Putney Bridge and Parsons Green LRT are within reasonable walking distance providing access to the District Line. Imperial Wharf station (National Rail) is within walking distance, also local bus routes are available from Wandsworth Bridge and New Kings Road. These economical offices/warehouse are offered on flexible lease terms.
